I designed and built Readflow on my own, including the app, the brand and the landing page.

[Website](https://readflowapp.com) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/readflow-speed-read-listen/id6762068804)

#### The idea

Most of the time you spend reading goes to moving your eyes across the page. Readflow uses RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) to show one word at a time in the centre of the screen. One letter in each word is highlighted in green. That's the Optimal Recognition Point, the spot your eye naturally lands on.

#### Features

- Import any PDF or EPUB, or paste in text
- Read from 100 to 800 words per minute and change speed on the fly
- Switch to audio mode and listen hands-free
- Daily goals, streaks, levels and a reading activity heatmap
- No account needed. The library stays on the device.

#### Design

The reader is the whole product, so everything else gets out of its way. The screen is black, the word is white, and green is used for one thing at a time: the letter you should look at, the progress you've made, or the button you should press.

#### What I did

- **Product, interface and brand design.** A dark palette with a single green accent, shared by the app and the site.
- **Mobile app** in React Native and Expo.
- **Landing page** in Next.js with a live RSVP demo.
- **Subscriptions** with RevenueCat.
